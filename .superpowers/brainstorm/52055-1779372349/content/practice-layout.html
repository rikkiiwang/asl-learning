Which structure fits best?

A

Camera-centric

Big camera is the hero; prompt in a header, feedback stacked below. Simplest, keeps self-positioning front and center. Hint appears under the camera.

B

Split (feedback + camera)

Read the hint and watch the example clip while seeing your own camera side by side. Best use of laptop width; a bit denser.

C

Guided single card

One focused card that changes by state (prompt → countdown → result → hint). Least overwhelming for true beginners; smaller camera, less info at once.
